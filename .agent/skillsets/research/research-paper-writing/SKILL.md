# Research paper writing

Before drafting, identify the audience, target venue, source format, final
rendered artifact, contribution, allowed claims, evidence artifacts, and any
current submission rules. Verify volatile venue requirements from official
sources instead of memory.

Maintain a claim-evidence map. Separate measured results, cited background,
interpretation, limitations, and future work. Do not invent experiments,
citations, author details, or compliance facts. Before calling a paper ready,
run `citation-verification` and obtain a fresh review with
`research-paper-review` when the host supports it.
